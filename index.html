How pricing works

Published menus. Customized quotes.

The menu and bar prices above are real and they don't move. What does move is everything around them — which is why your quote is built for your event rather than pulled off a shelf.

The room

Sabina on a Saturday is not Sabina on a Tuesday. The wine bar prices differently from a full buyout.

Day and time

Weeknights are our most flexible. Saturday nights in December are our least, and Sundays carry a premium — we open specially and bring in a full team.

Guest count

Minimums move with the room and the date. Larger groups often unlock better per-head economics.

What you need

Seated dinner versus a standing reception, a DJ, projection, a mixed platter format — each shapes staffing and setup.

How long it runs

Parties often go past the time they booked, which is welcome — it just adds to the final bill. Better said now than discovered later.

What's always added Every quote carries 10% DC sales tax, a 5% event fee and 18% gratuity on top of menu prices. We publish that here rather than at signature.
